# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to making participation in this project respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes respectful discussion, constructive feedback, empathy, and accountability. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository maintainer or project issue tracker. Reports should include relevant links, timestamps, and context.
